# Core Truth House Brand Infrastructure Agent Runbook

This project uses Claude Code subagents to turn one audience signal, founder problem, offer idea, or Facebook content opportunity into a strategic brand and conversion system.

## Purpose

The system helps Core Truth House create content and offers that are grounded in audience demand, aligned with the Core Truth House brand architecture, and connected to a meaningful business path.

The system must never function as a generic social media content generator.

Every output should help the audience move toward greater recognition, clarity, trust, and readiness.

## Workflow

1. The main Claude Code session acts as the coordinator.
2. The brand-signal-researcher analyzes:
   * Audience demand
   * Founder frustration
   * Visible symptoms
   * Structural brand problems
   * Audience language
   * Buyer psychology
   * Signal OS level
   * Opportunity score
3. The brand-architect turns the signal into:
   * Structural diagnosis
   * Positioning
   * Transformation promise
   * Message architecture
   * Offer architecture
   * Unique mechanism
   * Value ladder
   * Proof requirements
   * Objection handling
4. The facebook-content-strategist turns the architecture into:
   * A Facebook content concept
   * A complete Facebook post
   * A video hook
   * A Facebook video structure
   * A Facebook Live option when appropriate
   * Conversation prompts
   * Comment strategy
   * Visual direction
   * A content sequence
5. The conversion-path-builder creates:
   * The conversion asset
   * Facebook CTA
   * Comment pathway
   * Direct-message pathway
   * Follow-up sequence
   * Qualification logic
   * Sales path
   * Tracking recommendations
6. The coordinator combines the outputs into:

outputs/core-truth-house-brand-system.md

## Coordinator Rules

* Do not let one agent perform every job.
* Keep market and audience research separate from brand architecture.
* Keep brand architecture separate from Facebook content strategy.
* Keep content strategy separate from conversion design.
* Require a structural diagnosis before content is written.
* Require positioning before an offer is recommended.
* Require an audience stage before selecting a CTA.
* Require evidence, inference labels, and opportunity scores before finalizing.
* Every final output must support the Core Truth House positioning.
* Every final output must identify the audience's Signal OS level.
* Every final output must connect to an appropriate monetization path.
* Every final output must preserve the calm-authority voice.
* Every final output must help the audience understand that the visible issue may be a symptom of a deeper brand infrastructure problem.
* Do not automatically send every audience member toward the highest-priced offer.
* Choose the next step based on readiness.
* Do not measure success using reach alone.
* Prioritize recognition, meaningful conversation, qualified movement, and conversion.
* Avoid generic branding language.
* Avoid producing more content without clarifying the strategic purpose.

## Quality-Control Questions

Before completing the file, the coordinator must confirm:

1. Does the content reflect a real founder problem?
2. Does it identify the structural issue beneath the visible symptom?
3. Does the message sound like Core Truth House?
4. Does the content strengthen Shake's authority?
5. Is the central idea understandable quickly?
6. Does the Facebook post encourage a meaningful response?
7. Does the next step fit the audience's readiness?
8. Does the conversion asset extend the content naturally?
9. Does the offer belong within the Core Truth House ecosystem?
10. Does the system connect brand clarity to recognition, authority, opportunity, or revenue?
11. Is the promise credible?
12. Is the path simple?
13. Does the work avoid generic branding advice?
14. Does the content teach, diagnose, demonstrate, reveal, or reframe?
15. Would a serious founder see themselves in the message?
