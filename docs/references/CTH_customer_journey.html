Why this stage matters
The lead magnet is your first real offer. It converts attention into permission. A strong lead magnet is specific enough to attract the right person and valuable enough that they feel they owe you something in return.
Tip 1
Make it solve one specific problem, not everything at once
Tip 2
Name it so clearly the right person knows it is for them
Tip 3
The CTA should feel like the obvious next step, not a sale
